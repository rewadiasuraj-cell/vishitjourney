Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026

At Vishit Journey (vishitjourney.com), we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us when booking tour packages or visiting our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you fill out a booking inquiry, contact form, or make an advance payment, we may collect:

  • Full Name & Contact Details (Phone, WhatsApp, Email).
  • Travel Preferences, Destination Choices, and Group Size.
  • Transaction reference details via secure payment processors (Razorpay). We do NOT store credit card CVV or net banking passwords.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly used for:

  • Processing travel bookings, hotel reservations, and transport vouchers.
  • Sending booking confirmations via WhatsApp or Email.
  • Improving customer service and providing 24/7 travel assistance.

3. Data Security & Protection

We implement SSL encryption and secure servers to ensure your personal data is safe. We never sell, rent, or trade customer information to third-party marketing companies.
