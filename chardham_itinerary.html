DAY 06
Guptkashi → Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath
Kedarnath — The Sacred Trek
The most awaited part of the yatra. Drive from Guptkashi to Sonprayag, then to Gaurikund by local transport. From Gaurikund begins the trek to Kedarnath — on foot, pony or via helicopter as per availability. On arrival, check into your accommodation / dormitory and attend evening temple Darshan as per temple timings.

Important Information

⚠️

Char Dham Yatra is not a normal trip

  • At Yamunotri, the option of trek, pony or doli is available from Janki Chatti; at Kedarnath, the trek begins from Gaurikund.
  • Pony, doli and helicopter availability depends on weather and local rules, and are not included unless specifically mentioned.
  • Weather conditions in the Himalayan region can change road or temple access at short notice.
  • Applicable Char Dham registration must be completed before the yatra, and valid ID along with registration documents must be carried at all times.
